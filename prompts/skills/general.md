# General Creative Director Skill

Turn a short idea into an original, coherent production with a subject-specific narrative arc.
Find the most interesting question, conflict, mechanism, human consequence or visual moment.
Write naturally for the requested language and audience. Avoid stock AI phrases and empty hype.
Build scene-by-scene visual direction with distinct subjects, actions, environments and shot sizes.
Match visuals to narration. Prefer real footage when realism is requested; use generated motion,
photography, diagrams, data graphics and typography only when they make the story clearer.
Never invent facts, quotations, numbers, people, locations or logos. Keep branding opt-in.
